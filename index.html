01 // The Problem + Opportunity

Receding Urban Harvest Land
and Unused Rooftop Space

Bangladesh loses arable land, faces pesticide contamination, and suffers massive post-harvest losses — all while millions of rooftops sit empty.

📉

Declined Affordability

Vegetable production needs to increase 3× to meet current demand. Food price inflation peaked at 9.94% in 2023, eroding household purchasing power.

🌍

Arable Land Loss

Bangladesh has lost 2 million hectares of arable land over 22 years and is still losing 69,000 hectares every year to urbanisation.

☠️

Pesticide Misuse

Excessive pesticide use has caused 1.47 million hectares of land to lose 75% of productivity. 40% of washed vegetables still carry residue.

💧

Water Scarcity

Demand for fresh water will exceed supply in dry seasons by 2030. Fast-depleting aquifers signal a looming crisis.

📦

Fluctuating Wholesale Price

The 6-tier supply chain (farmer → faria → bepari → aratdar → retailer → consumer) inflates prices, degrades freshness and causes 23–44% post-harvest losses.

02 // The Big Idea

Rooftops → Hydroponic Gardens

An IoT & AI based Hydroponic Roof Gardening System. Leafy Farm → Wholesaler / Retailer — cutting down multiple tiers of middlemen, reducing transport cost and increasing shelf life to freshness.

Filtered nutrient solution pumped from central reservoir with constant EC/PH → distributed through feed tubes to root zone film → Leafy greens absorb precise nutrients → excess solution drains back and is reused.

04 // Differentiation & USP

Four Innovations — No Direct Competitor in Bangladesh

Leafy combines rooftop-native hydroponic infrastructure with a full-stack AI platform — a category that does not yet exist in the country.

01

AI-Powered Autonomous Control

AI-powered automated rooftop hydroponics with minimum human control. Full monitoring data sent to a web dashboard for real-time management — controllable from any device, anywhere.

02

Proprietary IoT Controller Box

Entirely Leafy R&D. Automates real-time EC/pH dosing, irrigation, and nutrient delivery with zero manual intervention. Field-tested across Dhaka rooftops. Every competitor in Bangladesh requires horticultural expertise.

03

Multi-Configuration Farm System

Three hardware formats — Indoor Farms (any crop, fully controlled), Outdoor Rooftop (semi-controlled, seasonal), and Pod (apartment-scale) — all run from one platform. Competitors offer only one format.

04

Distributed Rooftop-as-a-Platform

Rather than expensive centralised facilities, Leafy turns private rooftops into a distributed, scalable farming network — unlocking 180,000+ potential farm units in Dhaka alone at a fraction of conventional vertical farm capex.
